2. Architecture and flow

The design loop sizes every actuator option to the case, flies every mission mode with every option in the SILS, converges the sizing, sensors and algorithms, selects the lightest of our solution families that passes, dispatches its flight configuration and verifies it by Monte Carlo and in soft OILS. The same flight software (C99 and Rust, one pseudocode contract) runs in every rung behind one byte-level HAL.
