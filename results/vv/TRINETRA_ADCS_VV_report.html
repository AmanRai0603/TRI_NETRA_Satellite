2. Architecture and flow

The design loop sizes every actuator option to the case, flies every mission mode with every option in the SILS, converges the sizing, sensors and algorithms, selects the simplest of our solution families that passes, dispatches its flight configuration and verifies it by Monte Carlo and in soft OILS. The same flight software (C99 and Rust, one pseudocode contract) runs in every rung behind one byte-level HAL.

10. Open items

  • ais_3u: the selected mtq_fmr leaves budget: mass_kg 0.855 > 0.35; budget: volume_L 0.477 > 0.3. Either the case relaxes these (several are marked UNCONFIRMED in the case file) or the next design lever is needed.
  • Fine hold (imaging 3U): rate stability p99.73 is ~3.7e-3 deg/s against 1e-3 deg/s in every configuration and every campaign (MATLAB and engine alike): a requirement/controller-bandwidth conflict to resolve before OILS.
  • Fluid-loop field power: the 2 W electromagnet pump dominates the power of our FMR families; the permanent-magnet yoke removes it (the loop's power lever). It needs a measured prototype (IDMAS v2 §03C).
  • Sun-spin sign flip: a spin reversal instead of a hemisphere manoeuvre locks up in 3 of 24 seeds (identical in MATLAB, C and Rust).
  • Soft OILS models the OBC's CPU and buses; real OILS still needs the board target (fsw/targets/<board>/main.c: UART/Ethernet, clock, linker script, modelled on fsw/targets/qemu-mps2) and --realtime on adcs-link/1.
